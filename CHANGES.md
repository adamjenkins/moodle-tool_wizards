# Wizards 0.3.4

## Changed

- The question wizard and the question category wizard are no longer offered in a course's System
  shared question bank, and the wizards refuse to add questions or categories there. Teachers are
  asked to use a question bank activity of their own instead.

## Security

- Pictures of draft or disabled wizards are now shown only to people who can manage wizards. Pictures
  of enabled wizards are still shown to every logged-in user.
